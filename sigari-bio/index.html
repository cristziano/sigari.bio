What is sigari.bio?

A link-in-bio page built around your handle. You get sigari.bio/@yourname, and on it your links, socials and the look you choose.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything tagged live is free. Premium with extra effects and badges is in the works.

What can my @ be?

3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, numbers and underscores. Each @ belongs to one account.

Can I change my @ later?

Not for now. A claimed @ is locked to your account, so pick carefully.

How do I sign up?

With Google, or with email and password. Discord login is coming.

What are moods?

The same black and white look in three textures: smoke, dither and ASCII. Try them at the top of this page.
