faq

Questions.

What is sigari.bio?

A link-in-bio page built around your handle. You get sigari.bio/@yourname, and on it your links, socials, music and the effects you choose.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything you see on this page is free. A Premium plan with extra effects and badges is in the works.

What can my @ be?

3 to 20 characters: lowercase letters, numbers and underscores. Each @ belongs to one account only.

Can I change my @ later?

Not for now. A claimed @ is locked to your account, so pick carefully.

How do I sign up?

With Google or with email and password. Discord login is coming.

It says beta. What does that mean?

Some dashboard sections are still being built. Claiming your @ works today, and new features keep landing.
